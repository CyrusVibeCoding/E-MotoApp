# Design

Operate mode. Preserve the approved white, gray, black, and light-blue RepRide direction. White #ffffff surfaces, secondary #f2f6f8, ink #16242b, supporting text #53656d, borders #dce5e9, selected accents #196382, and light-blue #c3e8f8. Barlow Condensed headings and DM Sans body/bold reuse local font files from the approved preview.

Home gives the native map available space and keeps ride controls in a scrollable lower panel. Four standard bottom tabs stay reachable. Pages and forms use 22px spacing, 12-16px rounding, and 48-50px minimum controls. Larger-width content centers at 760px. GPS, saving, loading, error, permission, and empty states remain explicit. Native press feedback is immediate. Reference photography is labeled, and the build color diagram provides a structured interim presentation while partner 3D remains pending.
